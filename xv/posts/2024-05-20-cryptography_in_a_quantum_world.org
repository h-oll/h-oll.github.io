This is an informal rally for the Paris crypto x quantum community to
welcome Claude Crépeau as INRIA's International Chair Awardee.

The objective of the meeting is to: - get a sense of what everyone is
currently doing - identify and plan possible future collaborations

The format purposefully light and relaxed, with 2 to 3 30' talks per
session and at least 30' to chat freely after the talks.

* Location
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: location
:END:
The rally will take place in
[[https://what3words.com/streaks.begun.outdoor][amphi 55B at Jussieu]].
For speakers: It has large blackboards and a video projection system.

* Food
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: food
:END:

own.
